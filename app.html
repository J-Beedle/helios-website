About Helios Live

The Heliophysics System Observatory at any moment since March 2015: where each spacecraft was, the boundaries the solar wind set, and every mission's data a click away. Your browser reads it all straight from the public archives when you load a moment; no Helios server is involved.

Using it

Pick an event or a date and time (UTC) in the bar and press Load. Click any spacecraft for its data window, and Earth for the geomagnetic indices. Drag a window's bar to the top or a side of the screen to tile it; the green button opens it in a tab of its own. The time bar under the view moves the whole fleet. The page's address always describes what is on screen, so a copied link opens the same view.

What you are looking at

The view. OMNI's 1-minute solar wind sizes the magnetopause and the bow shock; the view names each model it draws. Frames come from MMS's ephemeris, or are computed here for moments it has not reached. Orbits are SSCWeb's. Field lines are traced through IGRF-14 and T89 at the moment's Kp; the auroral oval follows Starkov with the hour's AL.

MMS. FGM, FPI moments and spectra, HPCA, EDP and DSP: survey for four hours around the moment and burst within ten minutes of it, with electron, ion and HPCA distribution cuts on request.

Other missions. THEMIS and ARTEMIS, Cluster 1–4, GOES-16 to 19, ACE, Wind and DSCOVR, and OMNI.

Timing and accuracy. The numbers are the archives' current Level 2 versions, read at full precision where the archive allows and not checked by hand. A moment from the last few weeks opens with positions only until the missions publish their data.

Sources
